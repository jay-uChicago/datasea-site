How a pilot works

See the quality on your own data before you commit.

  1. Scope

    Send a sample of your data and what the model needs to learn. We draft labelling guidelines with you.

  2. Calibrate

    We label a small set together with your team and settle every edge case in the guidelines.

  3. Pilot batch

    We label a fixed batch at production settings and deliver it in your format.

  4. Quality report

    You get agreement scores, error examples and a price per item for the full volume.

Quality and data handling

Every batch is measured, not assumed.

Your data stays in the scope you set, and you see the numbers behind each delivery.

  • Double annotation on a sampleTwo annotators label the same items so agreement can be scored and disagreements reviewed.

  • Gold-set checksKnown answers are mixed into every batch to catch drift early.

  • Guidelines you ownEvery rule and edge-case decision is written down and handed over with the data.

  • Handled under Malaysia's PDPANDAs as standard, access limited to the assigned team, and personal data redacted where the task allows.
